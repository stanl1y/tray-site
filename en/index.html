How Tray worksPreparing for release · English & Traditional Chinese
01

Catch it on your phone.

Save a URL or some text from the share sheet. Add a next step if you already know what you want to do.

02

Pick it up from the menu bar.

Open Tray in your Mac menu bar and work through one item at a time. Shortcuts make capture and retrieval easy.

03

Done? Let it go.

Open, copy, or snooze. Done deletes the item. Everything expires after 30 days, so there is no collection to maintain.

for the in-between.

Offline? Save it locally first. Your iCloud connects the two devices. Background sync runs when the system allows it, or resumes when you reopen Tray.

Ordinary captures stay quiet. Choose “Remind me on Mac” when you want a single reminder.
