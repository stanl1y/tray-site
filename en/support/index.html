How do I save from my iPhone?

Install and open Tray once. In Safari or another app with sharing support, open the share sheet, choose Tray, and save. If Tray is missing, open More in the app row, then Edit to add it to Favorites. You make this change in iOS; share options can differ between apps.

I saved offline. Why is it not on my Mac yet?

A local save is separate from cross-device sync. Check that both devices use the same iCloud account and have a connection, then open Tray on your iPhone and Mac and check sync status. Retry if an error is shown. iOS schedules background work; reconnecting does not guarantee an immediate wake, especially after force-quitting. Keep the app and its data while troubleshooting so unsynced captures are not lost.

What does “device-only” mean?

Captures made before an iCloud account is verified stay on that device. Local test mode also does not sync across devices. Once an account is available, you must explicitly choose to add unassigned captures to it. Tray does not silently upload them to another account.

Does “Received on Mac” mean read?

No. It confirms that a Mac saved the original iPhone capture locally and returned its first receipt. It does not mean read, completed, notified, or that every later edit has arrived.

How do Mac reminders work?

Enable reminders in Tray on your Mac and allow Tray notifications in macOS. Ordinary captures do not notify you; “Remind me on Mac” requests one reminder. Tray waits at least about 10 seconds after the screen is awake and the session is unlocked. Sync can add more delay, and Focus or system notification settings can affect presentation.

What happens when I open, finish, or expire an item?

Opening, copying, and sharing keep an item. Done deletes it, with no trash or undo. Items expire 30 calendar days after creation; editing or snoozing does not extend that time. Expired items leave the inbox. Removal of content on offline devices and iCloud proceeds when sync can run. Move important information somewhere suitable for long-term storage.
