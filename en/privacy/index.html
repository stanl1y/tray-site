Privacy policy

2026-10-05 · Current prerelease app and this website

About this policy

This policy explains how the developer of Tray handles information associated with the current prerelease app, this website, and support communications. Tray is a temporary inbox between iPhone and Mac, not a permanent backup service. Last updated: October 5, 2026.

What you put in Tray

The URLs, text, titles, and next steps you choose to save are stored on your device, along with creation and expiry dates, snooze time, source platform, and reminder or first Mac receipt status. iCloud-enabled versions also sync this information using an Apple CloudKit private database. Tray has no developer-operated server receiving this inbox content and does not use it for advertising or marketing profiles.

Sync and accounts

Tray uses the iCloud account on your device. It does not create a separate Tray account or request your Apple account password. To separate local data for different iCloud accounts, the app uses a hash of an account record identifier supplied by Apple. Item and operation identifiers, a pending queue, and content-free deletion records help retries and prevent duplicates or deleted items reappearing. Captures made before an account is verified stay on the device unless you explicitly choose to sync them to a verified account.

Retention and deletion

Items expire 30 calendar days after creation; Done deletes them sooner. Opening, copying, or sharing does not delete them. Expired or deleted content no longer appears in the inbox. Removing cloud content requires the app to run and finish syncing. Offline devices, local caches, and system backups may not be cleared at the same time. Content-free deletion and operation identifiers may remain to prevent stale retries from restoring deleted data. Uninstalling the app does not immediately erase all iCloud data. Contact us for help with removal; do not include inbox content you do not want to share.

Content you open or share

When you open a URL, it is handed to your browser. When you use the system share sheet, the selected content is passed to the app or service you choose. Those websites and services process data under their own policies. The optional sharing practice in Tray opens example.com.

Export and import

You can explicitly export items available on the current device to a JSON file. Exports include content and original expiry dates, but not purchase entitlements, and are not sent to us. Files are not encrypted; choose a trusted location. Import requires confirmation and adds only missing, unexpired items. Known deleted items are not restored.

Diagnostics, testing, and purchases

The current version does not include third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs. Apple may provide app diagnostics or TestFlight feedback according to your system and testing settings; feedback you choose to send can include screenshots and contact information. The release candidate uses Apple StoreKit to verify trial and one-time purchase transactions, including product identifiers, original transaction dates, and revocation status. Entitlements are evaluated on device and are not sent to a developer server. We do not receive your card details. The iPhone app stores a verified grant in its private App Group so the share extension can check access offline. An app update or receipt change may require opening Tray and restoring purchases. Development builds do not charge users, public purchases are not yet available, and this website has no payment flow.

This website

This static site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no account login, tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or embedded contact form. GitHub states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes when visiting Pages sites. The support email link opens your email app; you decide whether to send a message.

Support messages and your choices

When you email us, we receive your sender address, message, and any attachments you choose to provide. We use Gmail to handle these messages and use the information to respond and troubleshoot. Please send only what is needed. We retain correspondence for resolving the issue and reasonably necessary follow-up support. You can email us to request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence, and we will handle requests under applicable requirements. You can also delete items in the app and manage iCloud and notifications in system settings. Take care of any unsynced local content before changing settings.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact diiinkservice@gmail.com. If our data practices change, we will update this page and its date, and provide notice in the app when needed.
